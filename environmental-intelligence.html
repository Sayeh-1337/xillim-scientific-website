Earth & Asset Intelligence

Environmental Change, Climate Exposure & Sustainability Evidence

Measure environmental change, not just claims.

We use Earth observation, environmental datasets and quantitative analysis to examine observable environmental change and climate-related risk—with transparent evidence quality and uncertainty reporting.

Discuss an assessment ↗ All services

What we can investigate

  • Long-term land-cover, vegetation and ecosystem indicators
  • Water-body, wetland, coastal and surface-temperature analysis
  • Historical temperature/precipitation trends and extreme-heat analysis
  • Drought, flood-hazard exposure and climate scenario screening
  • Satellite-derived atmospheric pollutant indicators where resolution permits
  • Environmental baselines and land-use impact assessment
  • Observable sustainability claim assessment with uncertainty reporting

Typical deliverables

  • Environmental timeline and exposure maps
  • Evidence and data-quality reports
  • Reproducible geospatial outputs
Scope and limitations: Satellite observations do not independently provide verified facility-level Scope 1, 2 or 3 carbon inventories or a complete ESG rating.
Typical clients: Investors, environmental consultancies, sustainability teams, real estate developers, infrastructure groups and research institutions.
