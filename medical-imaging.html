Advanced Medical Imaging & Quantitative Image Analysis

Quantitative imaging beyond conventional reporting.

We provide advanced medical image processing and quantitative analysis for biomedical research, clinical research, academic institutions, medical device development and medical imaging AI research—extracting reproducible quantitative information from existing medical images.

Discuss an assessment ↗ All services

What we can investigate

  • Quantitative Susceptibility Mapping (QSM) reconstruction and analysis
  • MRI relaxometry including T1, T2 and T2* mapping
  • Diffusion MRI and diffusion-derived metrics
  • Brain segmentation and regional volumetry
  • Longitudinal neuroimaging and anatomical change analysis
  • Organ and tumor segmentation, volumetry and lesion tracking
  • Medical image registration and 3D anatomical reconstruction
  • Radiomics, texture analysis and quantitative imaging biomarkers
  • Tissue characterization, QC and research-ready image datasets

Typical deliverables

  • Quantitative maps, measurements and segmentation masks
  • Imaging biomarker tables and statistical analyses
  • 3D anatomical models and scientific reports
  • Reproducible computational workflows
Scope and limitations: Research use only on authorized, appropriately de-identified datasets. Not an independent clinical diagnostic service.
Typical clients: Researchers, universities, hospitals, CROs, imaging laboratories and MedTech companies.
