Earth & Asset Intelligence

Satellite Image Analysis & Earth Observation Intelligence

Make the Earth’s observable history actionable.

By combining optical, multispectral, radar and environmental datasets, we reconstruct physical changes, detect anomalies and quantify geospatial patterns from publicly accessible and appropriately licensed Earth observation data.

Discuss an assessment ↗ All services

What we can investigate

  • Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 multispectral analysis
  • Landsat historical imagery and optical time-series
  • Cloud masking, atmospheric correction and geospatial alignment
  • Image classification, segmentation and change detection
  • SAR/InSAR deformation screening where data support it
  • Land-use/land-cover, urban expansion and construction change
  • NDVI, NDMI, NDRE and agricultural/water intelligence
  • Shoreline, water-body and large-area infrastructure monitoring

Typical deliverables

  • Change-detection maps, time series and GIS-ready datasets
  • Geospatial analytical reports and spatial statistics
  • Risk/anomaly maps and reproducible processing results
Scope and limitations: Remote sensing provides indirect measurements; site conditions require appropriate validation.
Typical clients: Investors, agricultural businesses, infrastructure groups, environmental consultancies and research institutions.
