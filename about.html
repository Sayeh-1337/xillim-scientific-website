FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with existing datasets?

Yes. We typically work with authorized exports, public Earth observation data and appropriately controlled interfaces—without requiring installation of a new platform.

Do you require software installation in our organization?

Usually no. Many engagements use secure data transfer of authorized exports. Where interfaces are needed, they are scoped carefully and do not replace validated systems.

Can you independently validate an existing AI model?

Yes. We provide technical benchmarking, robustness and generalization assessment. This is not regulatory certification unless separately authorized studies are in place.

Can you support pharmaceutical manufacturing without modifying validated systems?

Yes. Analysis uses authorized datasets or controlled interfaces; formal GMP decisions remain with the responsible organization.

Can you analyze publicly available satellite data?

Yes—for example Sentinel and Landsat—plus other appropriately licensed sources when available.

What is needed for an asset-risk assessment?

Typically site location or asset polygons, the hazard questions of interest, and any available contextual site information. Structural certification and legal due diligence remain outside satellite-intelligence scope.

How is confidentiality handled?

Share only non-confidential summaries at inquiry stage. Formal confidentiality arrangements can be agreed before sensitive data transfer.

How do pilot projects work?

We define a focused question, assess data fitness, deliver a scoped analysis with explicit methods and limitations, then decide whether to extend the engagement.
